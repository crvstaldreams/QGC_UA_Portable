QGroundControl-UA custom build overlay.

This build is based on QGroundControl and uses Qt and GStreamer. Upstream licenses remain applicable.

The portable build also bundles the Play typeface by Jonas Hecksher / Playtype from Google Fonts.
Play is distributed under the SIL Open Font License (OFL). The corresponding Play-OFL.txt
license is bundled in the generated third-party license directory.

Argos Translate is used only as a build-time tool to generate the Ukrainian Qt translation
catalogs and is not distributed as part of the QGroundControl portable runtime.

See the bundled third-party license directory in the generated package.
